## Rules
1. **42 px floor**: essential body text ≥ 42 px on a 1080-wide canvas (≈ 14 px when the post is shown 360 wide — the audience reads on phones). Marginal meta (URL, "SAMPLE" chip) may be smaller (16–24 px). Shorten or split into a carousel rather than shrink. Evidence: `dist/checks/social/post-1080x1350-360.png`.
2. **Margins** = 2 × the tile height: 72 px (1080², tile 72), 84 px (1350 / 1920). One vertical start line per post (RTL: the right edge).
3. **Safe zones**: stories / reels keep the top 250 px and bottom 300 px free of essential content (platform header, caption, actions); feed posts keep 72 px from every edge; square posts shown cropped to 4:5 on some feeds — keep the figure inside the central 1080 × 1350 → 1080 × 1080 is always safe.
4. **Logo**: dark posts carry the official tile (JPEG, clipped 22 %) with the name as text + "PART OF FIPER GLOBAL" (Inter 600, .06 em); paper posts carry the official horizontal lockup. Never the black-wordmark lockup on dark, never a retyped "FPT", never a glow.
5. **Red roles**: the tile and the CTA arrow only. Never red numbers, never red losses, never a red headline. Gold is not used (the site's gold "popular" badge is observed-only) — "الأكثر شعبية" is a muted text label.
6. **AR / EN**: Arabic headline first (IBM Plex Sans Arabic 700, letter-spacing 0), English secondary in Inter 500 muted; numbers in IBM Plex Mono, Latin digits, written LTR; the relationship line verbatim "Part of Fiper Global" / «جزء من Fiper Global».
7. **Data**: product facts only (sizes, fees, rules from the task file / `pricing.js`); any trader figure must come from `brand/scripts/sample-data.mjs` and carry the SAMPLE chip. **No performance claims, no invented statistics, no "x % of traders pass", no testimonials.**
8. **Regulatory**: nothing on the image. The caption carries the regulatory line once it exists — today a REQUIRED DECISION (`../legal/REQUIRED-DECISION.md`). Platform mentioned as cTrader only.
9. **Editing**: open the SVG in Illustrator / Figma / Inkscape with `brand/fonts/*.woff2` (or the Google Fonts TTFs) installed; keep `font-synthesis` off (no fake bold); re-export at 100 % — or re-run `node scripts/build-social.mjs` after editing the generator.
10. **Export check**: `scripts/build-social.mjs` fails if a referenced font family is not loaded at export or if the 1350 body falls below 14 px at 360.
